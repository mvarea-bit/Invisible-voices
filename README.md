INVISIBLE VOICES
### *Their voices deserve a future.*

🌍 **A digital platform for women's rights, awareness, and action.**

What happens when millions of women are denied the freedom to learn, work, and express themselves?

**Invisible Voices** aims to amplify the stories and aspirations of Afghan women and turn global awareness into meaningful action.

## 💜 Our Mission
Make Afghan women's voices visible, educate the world about their rights, and connect people with opportunities to make a difference.

## ✨ Key Features

📖 **Voices Archive** — Discover stories, dreams, and experiences.

🌐 **Beyond the Silence** — Explore verified information about women's rights and restrictions in Afghanistan.

🤝 **Take Action** — Find campaigns, educational initiatives, and organizations to support.

🛡️ **Safe Storytelling** — Prioritize consent, privacy, and the protection of contributors.

## 🔄 How It Works

**LISTEN → UNDERSTAND → ACT**

Discover stories. Learn about the challenges. Turn awareness into responsible action.

## 🛠️ Built For
A social-impact hackathon exploring how technology can support human rights and international solidarity.

## 🚀 Our Vision
We cannot promise to end oppression with an app. We can help ensure that these women are not forgotten and that their stories inspire meaningful action.

> 💜 *Their voices. Their rights. Our responsibility.*
