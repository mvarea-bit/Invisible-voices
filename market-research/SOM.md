# SOM — Serviceable Obtainable Market
## Invisible Voices

**Project:** Invisible Voices  
**Initial market:** Selected schools and educational communities in Ecuador  
**Planning period:** First 12 months after the initial pilot  
**Business model:** Educational and social-impact platform  
**Research date:** October 2026

## 1. Definition

The Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM) is the portion of the Serviceable Available Market that Invisible Voices could realistically reach during a defined period, considering its resources, development stage, distribution channels, and ability to attract users.

Unlike TAM and SAM, SOM should be based on a practical implementation plan rather than the total size of the education sector.

Invisible Voices is currently a prototype. Therefore, the project does not yet have verified adoption data, established school partnerships, or a measured user base.

## 2. Initial Market Strategy

The proposed first-year strategy is to test the platform with a small number of educational communities in Ecuador.

The project would prioritize:
- Schools interested in human rights education.
- Teachers who can introduce the platform to their students.
- Student groups working on social-impact projects.
- Users reached through educational presentations and digital sharing.

This approach allows the team to collect feedback, test the user journey, and improve the platform before attempting broader expansion.

## 3. Preliminary SOM Scenario

The following figures are **planning assumptions**, not observed market data or a verified forecast.

| Indicator | Preliminary target |
|---|---:|
| Educational institutions approached | 5 |
| Institutions participating in a pilot | 3 |
| Average participating users per institution | 100 |
| Total pilot users | 300 |
| Additional users reached through public sharing | 200 |
| Total first-year reach target | 500 users |

### Calculation

Pilot users:

3 participating institutions × 100 users per institution = 300 users.

Total first-year reach:

300 pilot users + 200 additional users reached through public sharing = 500 users.

The additional 200 users are a planning assumption and must be measured. The team should avoid counting the same person twice across school and public channels.

These figures represent an initial target for unique users reached, not guaranteed active users, paying customers, or completed learning outcomes.

## 4. Indicative Market Penetration

Using the Ecuadorian secondary education reference market of 1,791,353 students:

SOM penetration = (500 ÷ 1,791,353) × 100.

**Indicative penetration: approximately 0.028% of the reference secondary education market.**

This calculation illustrates the small scale of the proposed pilot. It does not establish actual demand or confirm that 500 users will be reached.

The calculation uses a broad national reference population; the actual percentage within the project's specific target schools could be different.

## 5. Implementation Plan

### Months 1–3: Preparation
- Review and verify the educational content.
- Test the prototype's navigation and accessibility.
- Prepare a short demonstration for schools and teachers.
- Identify potential pilot institutions.

### Months 4–6: Initial Pilot
- Approach educational institutions.
- Demonstrate the platform.
- Collect feedback from participating users.
- Record the number of unique users reached.

### Months 7–9: Improvement
- Fix usability issues.
- Improve the clarity and organization of content.
- Review source attribution and accessibility.
- Refine the outreach strategy.

### Months 10–12: Evaluation
- Measure the number of unique users reached.
- Review completion of key user tasks.
- Gather user feedback.
- Compare actual results against the 500-user planning target.
- Prepare recommendations for future expansion.

This timeline is proposed and should be adjusted to the team's capacity and the hackathon's requirements.

## 6. Key Performance Indicators

The project should measure:

- Number of institutions contacted.
- Number of institutions that agree to participate.
- Number of unique users reached.
- Number of users who complete the LISTEN, UNDERSTAND, and TAKE ACTION journey.
- Percentage of users who successfully locate educational resources.
- User feedback about clarity and usability.
- Number of content accuracy or accessibility issues identified and resolved.

Results must be reported only after they have been measured. Targets should not be presented as completed achievements.

## 7. Risks and Limitations

**Limited institutional access:** Schools may not have the time or resources to participate. The project should use flexible demonstrations and alternative online outreach.

**Low user engagement:** Visitors may leave before exploring the content. The team should test navigation, clarity, and the relevance of the material.

**Limited team capacity:** The team may not be able to maintain a large audience immediately. The first-year target should remain realistic and be revised based on evidence.

**Privacy and ethical concerns:** The platform must not promise secure or anonymous submissions unless suitable protections have been implemented. Pilot testing should avoid collecting sensitive personal information unnecessarily.

**Unverified demand:** The proposed target is a hypothesis to test, not proof that a market already exists for the platform.

## 8. SOM Conclusion

The proposed first-year SOM is a planning target of 500 unique users reached, beginning with a pilot involving three educational institutions and additional public outreach.

This limited initial scope allows Invisible Voices to test its educational value, improve its user experience, and build evidence before pursuing wider adoption.

The target should be reviewed after the pilot using actual user data, institutional feedback, and the team's available resources.

**Key limitation:** The SOM is an initial scenario, not a confirmed forecast. Actual reach will depend on partnerships, outreach, platform performance, and user engagement.
