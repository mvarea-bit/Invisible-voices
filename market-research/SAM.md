# SAM — Serviceable Available Market
## Invisible Voices

**Project:** Invisible Voices  
**Market:** Students and educators in Ecuador who could access digital human rights education  
**Geographic scope:** Ecuador, as the proposed initial market  
**Business model:** Educational and social-impact platform  
**Research date:** October 2026

## 1. Definition

The Serviceable Available Market (SAM) is the portion of the Total Addressable Market that Invisible Voices could realistically serve based on its initial geographic focus, intended audience, language, and distribution strategy.

For this analysis, Ecuador is considered the proposed initial market because the project can begin by reaching local schools, students, teachers, and educational communities before expanding internationally.

This is a proposed market-entry strategy, not a claim that the platform has already secured partnerships with Ecuadorian institutions.

## 2. Market Evidence

### Ecuadorian students

According to educational statistics presented by UNESCO's SITEAL country profile, Ecuador recorded **4,106,819 enrolled students in the 2024–2025 school year** across initial education, basic general education, and baccalaureate.

Source: SITEAL, Ecuador country profile  
https://siteal.iiep.unesco.org/pais/ecuador

### Ecuadorian educators

The same source reports **215,030 teachers** across these educational levels during the 2024–2025 school year.

These teachers represent a potential institutional audience for educational resources, classroom discussion, and human rights awareness activities.

### Secondary education

World Bank data sourced from UNESCO's Institute for Statistics reports **1,791,353 secondary education students in Ecuador in 2024**.

Source: World Bank Data, Secondary education students — Ecuador  
https://datos.bancomundial.org/indicator/SE.SEC.ENRL?locations=EC

The secondary education figure provides a more focused reference for a project initially designed to engage students and educational communities. It should not be added to the total student count because it is already included within the broader education population.

## 3. SAM Estimate

The broad Ecuadorian education audience provides the following reference figures:

- Total enrolled students across the reported school levels: 4,106,819.
- Secondary education students: 1,791,353.
- Teachers across the reported school levels: 215,030.

For an initial student-focused SAM, **1,791,353 secondary education students** can serve as a geographic and educational reference market.

This figure does not mean every secondary student is an appropriate target user. The actual SAM will depend on access to digital devices, interest in human rights education, curriculum relevance, language, and the ability to distribute the platform through schools or online channels.

Teachers and educational institutions are potential additional audiences, but they should be analyzed separately to avoid double counting.

## 4. Target Audience

The initial target audience in Ecuador includes:

- Secondary school students.
- Teachers who teach social sciences, citizenship, history, or human rights topics.
- Schools seeking digital educational resources.
- Student organizations interested in gender equality.
- Young people who engage with global social issues.

## 5. Market Access Strategy

Invisible Voices could reach this audience through:

1. Demonstrations at school events and student innovation competitions.
2. Presentations to teachers and educational institutions.
3. Sharing educational content through school communities.
4. Collaborations with organizations focused on human rights education.
5. Public access to the website through social media and educational networks.

These are proposed distribution channels. Their effectiveness must be evaluated through real outreach and user feedback.

## 6. SAM Conclusion

Ecuador provides a practical initial market for testing Invisible Voices because the project can begin within a defined educational environment and measure engagement before expanding.

The reference SAM is 1,791,353 secondary education students, based on 2024 data. This is a broad demographic reference, not a verified count of users who would adopt the platform.

Further research should identify relevant schools, teachers, curricula, digital access, and potential institutional partners to refine the serviceable audience.

**Key limitation:** SAM is an estimate of the audience the project could potentially serve, not the number of users currently reached or committed to using Invisible Voices.
